## Purpose, vision and mission

Help senior engineers, technical leaders and executives understand and evaluate how systems engineering changes through executable models, software-driven workflows and AI agents. Build informed support within the company through useful explanations and inspectable examples.

**Vision:** R&D teams investigate more possibilities, test ideas earlier and evolve useful systems through shorter feedback cycles, while developing the engineering environment that supports the work.

**Mission:** Explain and demonstrate how models, software-driven workflows and AI agents support that change across software, electronics, physical design and operation. Help readers understand mechanisms, evaluate results and identify a useful next experiment.

Credibility follows from substance and judgment. The site is an engineering publication and learning environment, not a service offer, hiring funnel, platform advertisement or demonstration of web-design skill.

## The engineering argument

Greenfield products and applications are the main setting. Use cases, requirements and architecture can be unclear; the team learns while making progress. Carry that story through implementation, integration, testing, validation, delivery and evolution.

> We develop, operate and improve the engineering environment alongside the product.

“We build the machine that builds the machine” makes this relationship memorable. The second machine includes people, methods, models, tools and workflows. Improve it through concrete product questions; reuse existing capability and add what the next investigation requires.

Show what teams can model, investigate, delegate, evaluate and deliver differently. Speed means shorter learning loops and more frequent useful improvements. Software releases, model revisions and physical prototypes have different cadences. Testing and quality are part of this work.

AI used during engineering and AI in products are distinct subjects. Digital threads and integration support the work; connected information alone is not the principal benefit. Product maturity and planning should reflect findings, uncertainties and dependencies as well as completed activities. Governance and assurance remain in the curriculum, proportional to the work they qualify.

## Vocabulary

The [curriculum map](CURRICULUM-MAP.md#terminology-and-introduction-sequence) owns the detailed definitions and introduction sequence.

| Term | Editorial use |
| --- | --- |
| Systems engineering | The whole-system scope across disciplines, people and use. |
| Executable engineering | The tutorial's working description: models, analyses, tests and parts of the workflow can be run, evaluated and improved. |
| Software-driven workflows | Software supports engineering work across disciplines, not only software development. |
| AI-assisted engineering | The broad category of AI used in engineering work, including interactive assistance and delegated tasks. |
| AI agents / agentic engineering | Agents use tools, inspect results and adapt steps toward a defined task. Engineers direct and evaluate the work. Prefer “AI agents” in introductory prose. |
| AI in products | AI contributes to product behavior. It need not be agentic or use an LLM. |
| Large language model (LLM) | A model technology behind many assistants and agents, distinct from their tools and workflows. Expand the acronym at first use. |

Introduce the shared language through Chapter 1's engineering situation. Chapter 7 develops agent mechanisms and delegation. Do not make the reader memorize a glossary before encountering a problem. A fixed workflow does not become agentic merely because AI helped create it.

## Agentic feedback and deterministic models — editorial direction (17 November 2026)

**Value proposition:** Executable engineering is not impressive because a deterministic software calculation returns the same output for the same defined inputs and state. That is normally an expected property, not a product benefit to market. A stochastic model can also be replayed with controlled seeds; some ML inference is deterministic under specified conditions. State the computation and environment precisely when the distinction matters.

**What changes engineering work:** A well-defined model, explicit behavior checks and fast tool feedback let an engineer or an authorized AI agent propose a change, execute it, detect a failed criterion, refine the solution and rerun the checks. The agent can manage a **bounded end-to-end flow**, rather than needing a person to issue every intermediate task. This is feedback for the current work, **not automatic retraining of the underlying AI model**.

**Two connected loops:**

- **Inner / executable feedback:** implementation, model execution, behavioral/invariant/regression checks, failure analysis and correction. Grant bounded agent autonomy where criteria are relevant, sufficiently independent of the proposed change and protected from unreviewed manipulation. Check valid and negative cases; a green build or rewritten expectation is not an engineering result.
- **Outer / engineering validity:** product intent, stakeholder needs, model fidelity, integration across disciplines, physical and operational evidence, safety/security implications, authority and change eligibility. Humans and suitable experimental/physical evidence resolve questions that the model's tests cannot answer.

**Jämförbarhet / comparability:** Keep this concept where it is meaningful: Chapter 05's data and experiment-design question asks whether two outputs have compatible metric definitions, units, configurations and conditions. A repeatable algorithm does not establish that two experiments may be interpreted as a controlled comparison.

**Cross-site consistency:** Homepage and Chapters 04, 05, 07 and 12 should lead with this capability/feedback story, not tool-selling phrases such as "comparable and reproducible" used without an actual engineering decision. The V3 **fictional four-way traffic intersection** is the core example; the older published V2 monitoring-device reader pilot remains a separate historical asset. Never imply that a sample agent workflow, physical interface experiment or qualified controller was executed when it was only proposed.

**Editorial guardrail:** Explain determinism once near the executable model. Thereafter show the decisions and capabilities that automated feedback enables. Use evidence boundaries at the claims they affect, without repeating broad AI disclaimers after every paragraph.
