## The correction

**WC-01 · P1 · high confidence.** In “Follow a queue into the second cycle,” the instruction says “Each scenario repeats its own eight arrivals and phases.” The T1 source has eight arrival **rows** per scenario. Summing those rows gives two north–south and two east–west vehicles per cycle. A reader who interprets the figure as eight vehicles per cycle would divide a completed two-cycle waiting total by sixteen. Chapter 6 correctly specifies eight vehicles for that entire cohort. The discrepancy matters especially at the Lab link, which lands on the figure. It also existed at B2.
