# How the intersection course carries a decision forward

[Published course][home] · [Curriculum map][map] · [Active manuscripts][index] · [Review contract][program]

The course gives a technical leader a worked method to choose a small investigation, distinguish the claim its result supports, and identify the dependency that determines when the complete change can be released. The strongest demonstration starts when the controller and adapter use the same word for different events. It ends with a release lead holding working software because a technician would reach the wrong instruction. Two sentences blur that demonstration. Chapter 12 gives the reviewer the product decision, and Chapter 14 describes a command receipt as an established event. Both can be corrected without changing the case or the chapter order. ## Two paths through the same fictional intersection

**Investigating a question.** Drivers report bursts and pedestrians ask who may turn while they cross. The city can investigate arrival placement with a two-queue model. Comparing the operations sketch with the model exposes an event-order disagreement. Once resolved, moving one north–south arrival from slot 3 to slot 2 in original B gives one vehicle waiting after slot 8, compared with two in original B. For two continuous cycles the team returns to the unedited patterns. B has two waiting north–south after both slots 8 and 16. Its north–south departure at slot 9 reveals that vehicles carried over. This is a reason to ask operations for arrivals aligned to phases. Crossing opportunities require different observations. No roadside improvement has been measured. [Workshop][c02] · [Moved arrival][c05-rows] · [Boundary observation][c06-cycle]

**Releasing a change.** The saved phase sequence gives the product team an expected amber request at one checkpoint. The proposed controller expects evidence of an applied indication. The proposed adapter promises receipt. An emulator could exercise the service display, a camera could observe a head on a bench, and a maintainer trial could test whether people understand the diagnostic. Maya defers acceptance of that interface pair. Chapter 13 deliberately assumes a separate configuration already authorized to report an unknown indication. Only clearer wording and a procedure for its existing diagnostic enter the expedited exercise. The release lead needs the identified running configuration, functional, access and performance checks, usable manuals in every supported language, logistics and training. The clock runs from acceptance of the eligible request until the lead records the complete candidate releasable. Deployment follows an operations decision. The one-day target is hypothetical. [Test oracle][c10-oracle] · [Maya’s decision][c12-dec] · [Release scope][c13-scope] · [Whole-product rehearsal][c13-check]

What crosses from one path into the other is specific. The phase order can supply a test expectation. Timing needs a physical clock, and the lamp needs an observer. In the other direction, a missing confirmation can change a display requirement, a test, an operator procedure and the next investigation. This gives the two feedback loops an engineering purpose. ## Findings for editorial decision

**CF-01 · Restore the decision owner at the Chapter 11–12 handoff. P1, high confidence.** The Chapter 12 phrase “Chapter 11’s reviewer deferred accepting the controller and adapter together” combines two roles. Chapter 11 assigns deferral to “the accountable product decision-maker” and the evidence request to the integration reviewer. The T1 role table agrees with Chapter 11. The discrepancy comes at the precise point the reader is asked to distinguish permission to run work from authority to accept a product. A reviewer can advise deferral without owning that decision. Replace that one sentence with “In Chapter 11, the accountable product decision-maker deferred accepting the two interfaces together. The integration reviewer asked for an independent observation of the signal head.” A shorter edit is “The product decision-maker deferred acceptance. The integration reviewer requested an independent observation.” Keep Maya’s introduction, retry table and quiz. Verify against the role table, then ask who can retry, who judges the observation method and who can accept the specific pair. The edit affects one paragraph and requires no fixture or quiz change.
