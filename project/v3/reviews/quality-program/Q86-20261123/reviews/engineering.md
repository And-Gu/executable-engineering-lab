### ENG-02 · Distinguish a complete mean wait from an aggregate the trace already supports

**P1 · High confidence.** Chapter 5 states “Mean waiting time would require following individual waits,” and the feedback for the wrong average-wait answer says it “would require following each vehicle.” Chapter 6 repeats the argument for mean wait. Chapters 7 and 9 separately ask about **individual** waits, for which tracking vehicle histories serves a genuine purpose. A final queue difference alone gives neither a mean nor a duration. The broad methodological claim can nevertheless send an engineer to collect identities or redesign a model before defining the statistic. [Chapter 5 interpretation][05mean] · [Chapter 5 quiz][05quiz] · [Chapter 6 experiment][06carry] · [Chapter 7 follow-up][07tdd]

As a counterexample, charge one abstract unit to every vehicle left waiting after service in each slot. Summing the eight combined queue counts gives 21 for original B and 15 for edited B. These are cumulative counts under an explicit discrete convention. They can be calculated without vehicle IDs. If a **new** comparison admits only those four vehicles and extends the fixed phases with zero fresh arrivals until both queues empty, the corresponding totals are 22 and 15. Each vehicle contributes once for each slot it waits, so division by four yields 5.5 and 3.75 modeled slots per admitted vehicle for that defined cohort. The empty-tail condition is my counterexample. It differs from the chapter's published two-cycle experiment, which repeats arrivals. No elapsed time in seconds is available. [Input and event order][inputs] · [Original trace][trace] · [Edited observation][curation]

**Proposed revision.** “One fewer vehicle at slot 8 tells us how many remain. To estimate a mean wait, first define whose wait counts, how to count waiting in a slot, and how to handle vehicles still queued when observation ends.” Mirror the distinction in the Chapter 5 distractor feedback and the Chapter 6 sentence. Keep the later individual-wait questions. The shorter alternative cuts the categorical method claim and retains the horizon warning. Publishing the additional arithmetic would consume attention in two already worked chapters. **Check.** A reader should be able to explain both why the last-row difference gives no mean wait and why summed queue occupancy can answer a separately defined question. No model change is needed.
