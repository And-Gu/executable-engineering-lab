# Reader observations

**Human learning outcomes are not observed.** No session records, quotations or task results have been collected for this study. Copy the private sections into agreed access-controlled storage outside Git before a session. Keep this maintained template blank. Use the [protocol and scoring key](protocol.md) for the chosen candidate.

## Round synthesis for editorial review

Fill this section only from consented human sessions. Record the round dates, candidate revision, course entry point, number of readers, broad mix and selection limitations. Use separate rows for materially different revisions, card versions and self-chosen versus supplied F scenarios. Do not publish per-person histories or combinations of details that could identify a reader.

| Card and version | Offered | Attempted | Independent | Completed with help | Not completed | Not attempted, offered then skipped | Not attempted, unoffered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

For each row, **attempted** equals independent + completed with help + not completed. **Offered** equals attempted + offered then skipped. Readers who start and stop count as attempted. Keep unoffered cards outside both denominators. For F, count unoffered F only once at card level. For any reported misunderstanding give the numerator and the number who received the relevant probe. Report missing probes and any help that limits interpretation. A small first round supplies no estimate of change in learning or time saved.
