## Review protocol

The **independent review was completed** on 2026-11-16 and is archived in [the full-course report](../../reviews/INDEPENDENT-FULL-COURSE-REVIEW-2026-11-16.md). Its criteria were:
1. The **whole course**: narrative, reader transformation, chapter order, repetition, omissions and cumulative example.
2. **Each chapter**: unique question, practical relevance, clear explanation, substantive example, purposeful learning experience, technically correct scope and useful bridge.
3. **Language**: natural, concise US English; cohesive paragraphs, helpful terminology, no formulaic chapter rhythm or repeated AI disclaimers.
4. **Visual/editorial design**: semantics before tools, graphics deriving from defined sources, early helpful diagrams, static-accessible alternatives and minimum necessary interactivity.
5. **Sources and validity**: separate simulated, assumed, physical, planned and observed evidence; quotations/statistical claims need primary verification.
6. **Document-as-Code lessons**: see [Independent review assignment](../../reviews/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW-BRIEF-2026-11-16.md) for actual source documents and evaluation rubric.

The reviewer is explicitly free to recommend **moving topics between chapters, renaming, merging, splitting or introducing new chapters** (including a revised chapter count), and the owner **subsequently approved fourteen chapters**. That approval changes the current structural authority, **not** the contents or approval status of these thirteen source drafts.

**Structural recommendation adopted by owner:** one integration chapter was added and later chapters reordered. Other independent recommendations remain proposals pending technical/editorial checks. Do not automatically rewrite manuscripts, declare a model executed, or publish chapters.
