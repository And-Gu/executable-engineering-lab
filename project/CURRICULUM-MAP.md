# Curriculum map — 12 chapters, four parts (v2.0)
Status: proposed. Routes below are targets; **do not assume they exist**.

| Part | # | Chapter | Distinct learning gain | Demonstration |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| I — Why and what next | 01 | The New Engineering Paradigm | Explain AI+digital engineering convergence | Fragmented vs connected workflow |
| I | 02 | Planning Under Uncertainty | Separate exploration from committed increments | DMS roadmap/option planning |
| II — Learn and specify | 03 | Engineering Knowledge as Code | Explain cross-discipline source relationships | Interactive change-impact graph |
| II | 04 | Learning Through Models and Prototypes | Qualify model fidelity and experiment results | Sensor/electronics/mount options |
| II | 05 | Spec-Driven Development Without Waterfall | Progressively specify the next slice | Hypothesis→spec decision studio |
| III — Act with agents | 06 | Directing AI Engineering Agents | Define agent scope, context, authority | Authentic run replay / contract |
| III | 07 | Engineering in Small, Reviewable Increments | Design meaningful, limited integration slices | Compare large PR vs split PRs |
| III | 08 | Continuously Integrated Engineering | Differentiate digital CI, hardware baselines and qualification | Multidiscipline integration dashboard |
| IV — Decide and scale | 09 | Verification, Validation and Engineering Evidence | Reason from claim to relevant evidence | Claim/evidence assessment |
| IV | 10 | Governance and Engineering Authority | Distinguish checks, permissions and approval | Review gate / authority simulation |
| IV | 11 | Managing Engineering Knowledge at Scale | Track configurations, reuse and supplier interfaces | Variant/baseline comparison |
| IV | 12 | The AI-Native Engineering Environment | Synthesize integrated workflow | End-to-end DMS engineering case |

## Content dependency model
01→02 sets motive and direction.
02 feeds 03, 04 and 05 through uncertainty priorities.
03 provides controlled context for 04, 05 and 06.
04 gives observations for 05 and decisions for 02.
05 prepares a bounded task for 06–08.
06–08 deliver reviewable results and expose gaps to 09–10.
09–11 inform new decisions and future roadmaps.
12 integrates all, including open decisions.
**These arrows do not imply waterfall or mandatory course navigation.**

## Narrative controls
- Planning, spec development, agents and review are core; do not subordinate them to V0 geometric explanation.
- Keep a reusable illustrative DMS storyline, but each chapter must move a real engineering question forward.
- Show feedback upstream to planning, hypotheses and interface decisions.
- Keep long-lead electronics/mechanical/physical-test cadences visible.
- Link existing V0 topics to optional sidebars/references where main narrative would become repetitive.
