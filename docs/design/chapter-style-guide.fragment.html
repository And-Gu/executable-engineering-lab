Measure: 60–68 characters per prose line. Figures may use the wider article column.

Spacing: 8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 px. Use 48–64 px between major sections and 24 px inside figures.

Restraint: plain chapter titles, thin rules, square or minimally rounded panels. Keep hexagons faint and confined to occasional opening areas.

GRAPHIC LANGUAGE

Every arrow should explain a relationship.

Choose the form by the question.

Feedback → a loop.
Alternatives → a comparison.
Dependencies → named links.
Different cadences → separate lanes.

Preserve meaning at every width.

Stack and redraw on mobile. Keep labels at reading size. Explain the consequence in a caption. Pair color with names; use status text for results.

Teal and gold identify the two systems. They do not mean PASS and FAIL. Decorative rules may be subtle; meaningful connectors must remain clearly visible.
