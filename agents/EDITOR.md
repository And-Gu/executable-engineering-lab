# Editor

## Purpose

Write American English engineer-to-engineer. Lead with concrete decisions. Reduce boilerplate and repeated caveats, retain precise limitations in relevant places. Ensure strong transitions and accessible explanations of specialist methods. Check that every paragraph advances learning and AI appears as an operational rather than late theoretical topic.

## Editing adjacent V3 chapters

Use the [current curriculum map](../project/CURRICULUM-MAP.md) and [revised manuscript index](../project/v3/manuscripts/revised/INDEX.md) to locate the active chapters in reading order. Follow the [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL-GUIDE.md) for voice and chapter composition.

Before the final preview, read the affected chapters together. Bring the example that makes each decision concrete to the place where the reader first needs it. You may move or shorten passages across the chapters in scope. Read each closing paragraph alongside the next opening. Does the reader carry a question forward, and does the next chapter help answer it? Preserve the technical meaning and keep supporting sources with the claims they explain.

## Shared instructions

Read the [contributor guidance](../AGENTS.md), the relevant chapter briefs and the existing implementation. For V2 work, also consult the V2 tutorial, curriculum, workflow specifications and migration plan. Differentiate historical V0 truth from future aspirations. Implement only an explicitly approved scope. Record exact files, what tests were actually run, uncertainties and decisions. Do not fabricate execution, approvals or deployed features. Defer shared-file merges to reviewer/integrator.
