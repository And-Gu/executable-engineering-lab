**R14. Activity, release and use.** Demirer, Musolff and Yang’s May 2026 NBER working paper, revised September 2026, used a matched observational event study of public GitHub work. Table 6 in §6.1 shows larger estimated increases in commits than in releases after adoption of synchronous agents. These counts leave coding costs and industrial returns unmeasured. In the descriptive marketplace comparison through August 2026, Figure 11 tracks each new cohort’s first three months. iOS ratings stayed roughly flat, Android downloads rose modestly and Chrome downloads fell along an earlier trend. Sparse downloads precluded a SourceForge usage comparison (footnote 30). Competition for users’ attention could explain why usage lagged entry (§7.2.4). Place the paper beside the companion’s pull-request counts. [Revised working paper](https://doi.org/10.3386/w35275)

**R12. Who pays for upkeep?** DORA’s mixed-methods 2025 report (v. 2025.2, pp. 38, 67, 103, 114) associates higher AI adoption with both higher software delivery throughput and greater instability. The 4,867 survey participants received different question sets. The platform figures describe respondents’ organizations. Ninety percent had an internal platform and 76% had a dedicated platform team. Qualify the companion’s opening claim about project size as a proposition the solo author can test. Keep its monthly bulletin comparison. Weigh setup, hosting, model use, review and repairs after a spreadsheet column changes against repeated searches and corrections avoided. **R18. Buyer expectations.** The UK Infrastructure and Projects Authority’s September 13, 2021 policy required public infrastructure clients in scope to specify required information and its satisfactory delivery in contracts and to receive and assure it digitally. This requirement predates current coding agents. Use it as optional reading under the companion’s 2026–2030 hypotheses. A customer who benefited from a faster handover may ask for the same turnaround next time. Check successive tenders for a tighter deadline at equivalent quality and ask the buyer what prompted any change.
