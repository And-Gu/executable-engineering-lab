# When the traffic engineer’s question changes

Research note EEL-R-Q86-05. Publication status `background-only`. See the research index for topic, chapter mapping and source status.

Chapter 7’s reviewed brief asks whether either directional queue grows between the ends of two continuous cycles. In scenario B, two north–south vehicles enter slot 9 already waiting. One departs on green despite zero new north–south arrivals. The final counts alone could come from two runs restarted from empty. This boundary observation gives the agent a way to detect a reset before asking the engineer for help. [Chapter 7, “The agent brief,” B1](https://github.com/And-Gu/executable-engineering-lab/blob/64931d4de37694f98e7590a02086ce43ec5ebbf2/project/v3/manuscripts/revised/07-spec-driven-development-without-waterfall.md#the-agent-brief)

GitHub’s Spec Kit v1.4.4, released November 23, 2026, guides software feature development. Its full workflow asks clarifying questions before planning. The shorter workflow omits the dedicated clarification and checklist steps. For an existing feature, the authors distinguish a defect from a change in agreed intent. A team can revise its specification or open a new one and must reconcile affected plans and tasks. In a June 23 maintainer reply, Manfred Riem explained that a custom checklist assesses the requirements’ wording and that reviewers can skip it for a small, unambiguous feature. The guide and reply share a project source. The slot 9 observation supplies the separate evidence about model behavior. [Quickstart, “Recommended Process”](https://github.com/github/spec-kit/blob/c8133f463a586879e3af6dfd2864beb8995a8764/docs/quickstart.md#recommended-process) · [“Assess the Change” and “Make the Revised Intent Explicit”](https://github.com/github/spec-kit/blob/c8133f463a586879e3af6dfd2864beb8995a8764/docs/how-to/evolving-specs.md#assess-the-change) · [Riem’s reply](https://github.com/github/spec-kit/discussions/2387#discussioncomment-17411328)

If the runner must ask permission to restore the named input after every mix-up, reruns can queue behind a busy engineer. Chapter 8 already lets the coordinator restore the agreed input. A dispute over whether a vehicle can depart on arrival warrants the engineer’s judgment. Independently, Lauren Tan described moving a recurring Electron import decision into a lint rule. Her November 11 account describes one team. Estimating review delays here would require timing the lab’s own handoffs. [Chapter 8, slot 2](https://github.com/And-Gu/executable-engineering-lab/blob/64931d4de37694f98e7590a02086ce43ec5ebbf2/project/v3/manuscripts/revised/08-directing-ai-engineering-agents.md#supervise-by-exception-not-by-reading-every-agent-message) · [Tan’s 5:00 chapter guide](https://ai.engineer/talks/O_zcc6Xl12g-all-code-is-slop-except-mine) · [Livestream transcript, 1:07:34 to 1:08:18](https://ai.engineer/code/2026/talks/lauren-tan)
