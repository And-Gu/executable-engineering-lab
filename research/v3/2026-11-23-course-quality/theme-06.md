## Editorial choice

Consolidate Chapter 9’s checkpoint into observations and next checks. Distinguish the wrong B revision by its slot 2 arrival and departure. Expose a hidden east–west reset with vehicles waiting at the boundary. Escalate a disputed definition or a request for two independent windows to the engineer. Expand delegation for the demonstrated task class once a deliberate error fails an independently grounded check. In Chapter 14, let the agent propose a check and the responsible engineer judge its criterion against a separate observation. Keep the companion recording.

A smaller edit would add one question at Chapter 9’s handoff asking which deliberate error the check catches and who resolves a disagreement. It tests the delegation decision but leaves revision diagnosis implicit.
