OMG adopted SysML 2.0 in September 2025. The March 2026 Part 1 specification (`formal/2026-03-02`) defines textual and graphical notations in §8.2.1 and the rules for selecting exposed elements in §8.3.26.11. Its informative overview in §7.26.1 explains that a rendering can preserve correspondence with model elements. A view could omit a detector input because of its selection conditions. An assistant’s sketch could also introduce an input that the team has yet to define. [Formal version record](https://www.omg.org/spec/SysML/2.0/About-SysML) · [Language specification](https://www.omg.org/spec/SysML/2.0/Language/PDF)

Under Clause 2, a tool can conform through abstract syntax and model interchange even if it cannot run the model. Semantic conformance is optional and can be demonstrated through analysis or execution. The companion OMG Systems Modeling API and Services 1.0 specification (March 2026, `formal/2026-03-04`), §7.2.2, defines retrieval of incoming or outgoing relationships at a specified project commit. If a team encoded such a relationship in a SysML project, this service could locate it by direction. Computing the queue requires the behavior and its inputs. Both specifications come from OMG. Independently, #87 R02, the June 2018 U.S. DoD *Digital Engineering Strategy*, sets governance of lifecycle models and data as an objective in Goal 2, §2.2, p. 9.
