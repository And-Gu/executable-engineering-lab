## Proposed reader-facing demonstration — Engineering Data Investigation

This manuscript is the editorial source for a future v2 browser experience. The following is a design contract, **not a claim of completed functionality**.

Use an immutable V0 parameter-study dataset generated from the shared coverage calculation. Add a separate small, explicitly synthetic variant-constraint fixture with fictional power and mounting assumptions. Let readers inspect original values; apply deterministic annotations and joins keyed by versioned configuration identifiers; compute margins and an inspectable finite-difference sensitivity metric; switch among raw, curated, and derived tabular views; and investigate a single decision question through linked plots.

Make mismatched identifiers visible rather than matching by name. Provide an alternative data table for every plot. Preserve original source definitions and separate temporary browser calculations from recorded execution evidence. Do not alter the canonical V0 system, assertions, or reference FAIL result.

The new demonstration must provide a fresh learning experience, rather than a repetition of the old position/radius slider.
